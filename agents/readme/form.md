# PlainPath Form

Explains official government forms and the documents you need, **in plain words and in your language**.

- Uses forms and document lists extracted from the official website by the PlainPath Gov Extractor Apify Actor,
  including the fillable field names of PDF forms (for example California DMV REG 195 and REG 195A).
- Bitdeer AI Model Studio explains what each form is for and what to write in its most important fields.
- Every form and document links to the official source. Nothing is invented: if the official pages list no forms,
  it says so.

Try: "Which forms do I need to renew a disabled parking placard?"

Information comes from the official website; always check the original. No legal or medical advice.

Part of PlainPath (Navigator, Crawler, Answer, Form agents).
